Privacy Policy

XS Connect by Xelorsoft. Last updated 16 November 2026.

XS Connect is software made by Xelorsoft, India. Businesses ("brands") use it to answer their customers on WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook Messenger, with help from an AI assistant. This policy explains what we collect, why, who processes it, and how to have it deleted.

What we collect

  • Brand accounts: the name and email of people who sign up, their brand's name, and the business details they enter (products, prices, opening hours, answers to common questions).
  • Messages: when a brand connects a WhatsApp number, Instagram account or Facebook Page, we receive the messages its customers send to that account and the replies sent back. This includes the customer's name and phone number or account ID as Meta provides them, text, voice notes, and delivery status.
  • Sales records: leads, orders and follow-ups created from those conversations.
  • Technical data: basic logs needed to run and secure the service.

How we use it

  • To show a brand its conversations and let its team reply.
  • To let the AI assistant reply on the brand's behalf, using only that brand's business details and that conversation.
  • To turn voice notes into text so they can be read and answered.
  • To bill brands for usage and keep the service secure.

We do not sell personal data, use it for advertising, or use one brand's data to answer another brand's customers.

Who processes it for us

  • Meta Platforms (WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger): delivers messages between the customer and the brand.
  • Supabase: stores the data, in its Mumbai, India region.
  • Google (Gemini API): generates AI replies and transcribes long voice notes.
  • Sarvam AI, India: transcribes voice notes.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the XS Connect website.

These providers process data only to provide their service to us.

Data from Meta platforms

Data we receive through Meta's platforms is used only to provide XS Connect to the brand that connected the account, as described above, and in line with Meta's Platform Terms. A brand can disconnect an account in XS Connect at any time; we then stop receiving its messages.

How long we keep it

We keep a brand's data while its account is open. When a brand or a customer asks us to delete data, we delete it within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep a record.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Each brand can see only its own data. Access tokens for connected accounts are stored where only our servers can read them.

Your choices

If you messaged a business that uses XS Connect, that business decides how your messages are used; you can also ask us directly. To see, correct or delete your data, follow the steps on our data deletion page.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
